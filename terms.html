Statutory & Ecotourism Regulations

Terms of Expedition & Forest Protocols

Operating in accordance with the Wildlife Protection Act (1972) and Sundarban Tiger Reserve (STR) Directorate guidelines.

1. Scope of Operations & Territory

Sundarban Wild Escape operates exclusively within the Indian sovereign jurisdiction of the Sundarbans (comprising the South 24 Parganas and North 24 Parganas districts of West Bengal, India). Under no circumstances do our voyages enter or reference Bangladeshi waters (such as Kotka, Hiron Point, or Mongla).

All embarkations and disembarkations take place via authorized ghats (primarily Godkhali Ghat). Forest entry is restricted strictly to designated ecotourism zones cleared by the West Bengal Forest Department.

2. Mandatory Identification & Government Permits

Entry into the Sundarban Tiger Reserve is a statutory privilege granted solely by the Directorate of Sundarban Biosphere Reserve and STR authorities.

  • Domestic Guests: Must provide a clear government photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License) at least 24 hours prior to travel.
  • Foreign Nationals: Must furnish valid Passport bio-data page copy and active Indian Visa / OCI card details minimum 48 hours prior to allow clearance at the Canning Forest Divisional Office.

Government permit clearance fees are disbursed directly to the Forest Department treasury and are strictly non-refundable once issued.

3. Wildlife Sighting Reality & Code of Conduct

Sundarban Wild Escape is a conservation-first safari outfit. In compliance with international ethical standards and Indian wildlife jurisprudence:

  • No Sighting Guarantees: We never guarantee sightings of the Royal Bengal Tiger or any specific apex species. Sightings depend on natural animal movements, lunar tides, and forest silence.
  • No Baiting or Chasing: Baiting, artificial calling, or steering vessels to obstruct swimming wildlife is strictly forbidden.
  • Complete Silence in Buffer Creeks: Guests must maintain low speaking volumes when navigating narrow estuarine canals (Pirkhali, Chokhakhali, Sundarkhali).
  • Zero Waste & Plastic Ban: The Sundarbans is a legally designated Zero-Plastic Zone. Disposing of plastics or garbage into rivers is punishable by immediate fine and expulsion from the reserve.

4. Tidal Dynamics, Weather & Force Majeure

The delta is governed by lunar gravitational tides with water levels fluctuating up to 4.5 meters. Boat routing, jetty berthing times, and watchtower arrival sequences are subject to modification by our licensed captains to ensure vessel draft clearance and passenger safety.

In the event of extreme meteorological conditions (such as severe cyclonic warnings issued by the India Meteorological Department - IMD or river navigation suspensions decreed by the Inland Waterways Authority / Forest Department), trips may be rescheduled.
