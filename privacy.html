Guest Data Protection

Privacy Policy & Forest Permit Security

Committed to the highest standards of data integrity in compliance with Indian digital privacy benchmarks and Forest Department statutory mandates.

1. Information We Collect

To plan and execute authorized ecotourism journeys into the Sundarban Tiger Reserve (STR), we collect personal contact details (name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number) and statutory identification details (Aadhaar, Voter ID, or Passport information).

2. Forest Department Statutory Disclosure

Under West Bengal Forest Department rules, entry manifests containing each guest's name, age, nationality, and government ID number must be registered with the Range Officer at Sajnekhali / Canning prior to river transit. We submit your documentation strictly to authorized forest officials for lawful permit issuance.

3. Zero Data Commercialization

We never sell, rent, lease, or distribute your email address, telephone number, or travel preferences to third-party marketing brokers or advertisers. All reservation communications are handled directly between you and our core operations team.

4. WhatsApp Communications

When you click our WhatsApp booking buttons or submit our inquiry form, you initiate a direct encrypted chat with our verified concierge number (+91 9564846891). WhatsApp’s end-to-end encryption protocols govern the transit of your text messages.
