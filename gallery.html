Our Field Photography & Wildlife Ethics

Every frame in our visual archive is captured during certified ethical safaris. We strictly adhere to the Sundarban Tiger Reserve Wildlife Act: no playback bird calls, no animal baiting, no artificial spotlighting, and respectful standoff distances from mudflat predators.
