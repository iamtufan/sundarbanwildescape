Transit & Coordinates

Journey from Kolkata to Godkhali Ghat

Godkhali Ghat is the roadhead of the Indian Sundarbans. Choose your preferred mode of transit below.

Total Distance 85 km
Transit Duration 2.5 to 3.5 Hours
Recommended Route Baruipur → Canning → Godkhali

The most scenic and comfortable option. Travel via the Eastern Metropolitan Bypass toward Baruipur, continuing along State Highway 3 to Canning, and finally crossing the Matla bridge towards Godkhali Ghat. Secure, paid multi-level vehicle parking is available at Godkhali under CCTV surveillance if you choose to drive yourself.

Train Segment Sealdah → Canning
Train Frequency Hourly Trains
Canning to Godkhali 28 km (Shared Auto / Taxi)

Suburban local trains depart regularly from Sealdah South section to Canning Station (approx. 1 hr 15 mins). From Canning Station, shared autos, Toto electric rickshaws, and private taxis make the 28-km road journey to Godkhali Ghat in roughly 40 minutes.

Vehicle Type Force Urbania / Innova
Amenities AC, WiFi & Sanitized
Departure Point Kolkata Science City

Our luxury group conveyance departs at 6:30 AM sharp from designated central Kolkata pickup points. Includes light breakfast packets, chilled water bottles, and experienced highway drivers.
